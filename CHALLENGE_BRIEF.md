## The Archive Awaits

The world moved on.

June 2026. Stark Industries' authentication infrastructure collapsed under coordinated assault. Response teams isolated the damage. Systems fell dark.

But in the wreckage of emergency procedures, something was forgotten.

**The E.D.I.T.H. fallback** — an emergency bridge, 48 hours of crisis management. Quick fixes by exhausted engineers. Scheduled for immediate decommissioning. Six weeks passed. Nobody noticed it was still running.

When discovery came, the attackers had already vanished. What remained: a backup archive from the compromised build server. Encrypted. Layered. Encrypted again.

Inside sleeps:

- System logs that whisper builder secrets
- Binary artifacts forged in haste
- Blueprints encoded in light itself
- Cryptographic keys scattered across multiple layers

The archive is self-documenting. It contains what you need—but only if you understand how to listen.

Your task: **Repair broken tools. Extract encrypted layers. Authenticate against a live system. Solve mathematics. Decrypt the flag.**

Each breakthrough enables the next. The portal is live. The server is watching.

## What You Hold

**Two files. One journey.**

**auth_backup.sba** — 8.7 MB of forensic remains from the compromised build server. Contains logs, binaries, visual artifacts, and guidance encrypted within.

**sba_extract.py.broken** — A recovery tool with three corrupted functions. To proceed, you must understand what each does, repair the implementation, and execute with precision.

## The Path Forward

1. Study the broken extraction script. Three functions are damaged. The code itself contains hints about what's missing.

2. Repair the implementations. RLE decompression, RC4 decryption, and key derivation all depend on cryptographic principles you must understand.

3. Extract the archive. Six artifacts emerge. One contains a message from someone who knew the system intimately. Read it carefully.

4. Navigate to the authenticated portal at **<http://134.209.148.23/>**. The system measures your progress. Each checkpoint gates access to the next layer.

5. Follow the breadcrumbs. The archive tells you what to do. The portal validates whether you understand it.
